Terms of Service

Last updated November 2026

1. About Tokea

Tokea helps people find and host free, one-time activities in Kenya. BizConnect provides the app. We do not organise, supervise or attend activities.

2. Who can use it

You must be at least 18 years old to use Tokea, as a member or a host. Hosts must also be verified. You must give accurate information and keep your account secure.

3. Your responsibility and safety

Activities are organised by other users. Do your own checks before joining any activity: read the details, meet in public places, tell someone where you are going and leave if you feel unsafe. You join and host at your own risk. To the fullest extent allowed by law, BizConnect is not responsible for anything that happens at or because of an activity.

4. Hosting rules

Hosts must be verified, describe their activity honestly, keep it free of charge, and act as the moderator of their chat. Hosts must not use activities to sell products, collect money or promote anything illegal.

5. Content rules

No sexual, vulgar, hateful, threatening, harassing or illegal content in English, Swahili, Sheng or any other language. Activity titles, descriptions and display names are checked automatically before they appear, because anyone can see them. Chat is moderated by its host and by reports from members. We may remove any content or suspend accounts that break these rules.

6. Reporting and enforcement

You can report activities, messages and voice notes in the app. Voice notes are held to the same content rules as everything else; if you have several messages reported or removed in an activity, voice notes are switched off for you there until we review it. We may suspend or remove accounts at any time for safety or rule violations.

7. Cancelled activities

Hosts may cancel an activity. Members are notified and can create a new activity that others can join.

8. Changes and contact

We may update these terms and will tell you about important changes. Questions: bizconnectkenya@gmail.com.
