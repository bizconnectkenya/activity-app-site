Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026

1. What we collect

Phone number or email, name, date of birth, county, the activities you host or join, your chat messages and voice notes, meeting points you choose to share, your reports, and a notification token for the phone you sign in on. Hosts also provide their legal name, a selfie and a photo of an ID document for verification; we record the ID type and number from it (stored encrypted).

2. Why we collect it

To run your account, show activities near you, enforce age limits, let members chat, send you notifications you have asked for, keep the community safe and verify hosts.

3. Your location

When you look for activities near you, your phone sends its approximate position with that one request so we can sort activities by distance. We do not store it and we cannot see where you are at any other time. You can refuse the location permission and still browse by county, sub-county, ward or area.

4. Verification photos

Your selfie and ID photo are stored privately and are only seen by our review team. They are never shown to other users. They are permanently deleted as soon as your request has been reviewed (approved or not). We keep only your legal name, the ID type and number (encrypted), the result and the date.

5. Voice notes

A voice note is stored privately and can only be played by the people in that chat. Our review team can listen to one only if it has been reported. Voice notes in an activity chat are deleted 3 days after the activity ends, when the chat closes; voice notes in a private chat are deleted after 30 days. Anything you delete, or that a host or our team removes, is deleted sooner. The recording stays on your own phone only until it has been sent.

6. Sharing a meeting point

If you share a meeting point in a chat, we work out how far away it is and show the people in that chat a description such as "300m away, at Jamhuri grounds". We never show a map or a link to your exact position. You can stop sharing at any time, which deletes the position itself straight away. A meeting point shared in an activity chat expires when the activity ends; in a private chat it expires after an hour. Never share your home.

7. Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a token that identifies the app on your phone so we can send them. It is not linked to your phone number and we remove it when you log out. You can switch off any kind of notification, or pause all of them, under Notification settings, and you can turn them off for the whole app in your phone settings.

8. What others can see

Other users can see your display name, your verified tick (if any), and your messages, voice notes and shared meeting points in activities you share with them. They cannot see your phone, email, date of birth, ID or your location.

9. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We use trusted service providers (our database host, our notification sender, and SMS/email senders) to run the app.

10. How long we keep it

If you delete your account, it is closed immediately and your profile is hidden. For safety and legal reasons we keep a private record (your names, contact details, the ID number recorded during host verification, activities, messages and reports) for 12 months, then delete it permanently. Voice notes, shared meeting points and notification tokens are deleted on the shorter timetables above. We only share records when required by law, for example a court order or a formal police request.

11. Your rights

You can edit your profile in the app and delete your account and data from the Me tab at any time. You can also contact bizconnectkenya@gmail.com about your data under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

12. Adults only

The app is only for adults aged 18 and over. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, email bizconnectkenya@gmail.com and we will remove it.

13. Contact

BizConnect, bizconnectkenya@gmail.com.
